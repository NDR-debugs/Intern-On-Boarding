# Story 1: Publish availability slots

**As a** Tutor  
**I want** to publish blocks of time when I'm available to teach  
**So that** students can find and book me without back-and-forth messaging  

## Acceptance Criteria

- **Given** I am on my availability calendar **when** I mark a 1-hour block as available  
  **then** it becomes visible to students searching for my subject

- **Given** I have already published a slot **when** it gets booked  
  **then** it is automatically removed from the searchable pool

- **Given** I publish overlapping slots **when** I save  
  **then** the system warns me of the overlap before confirming

## INVEST self-check

- [x] **Independent** — This feature can be developed separately without depending on other stories.
- [x] **Negotiable** — The exact calendar design and slot management rules can be adjusted.
- [x] **Valuable** — It allows tutors to provide availability and helps students book sessions.
- [x] **Estimable** — The requirements are clear enough for development effort estimation.
- [x] **Small** — The scope is limited to creating and managing availability slots.
- [x] **Testable** — Each acceptance criteria can be verified through testing.

# Story 2: Search for a tutor by filters

**As a** Student  
**I want** to search for tutors by subject, grade, language, and price band  
**So that** I can quickly find a tutor who fits what I need and can afford  

## Acceptance Criteria

- **Given** I am on the search screen **when** I set subject = "Combined Maths" and language =
  "Sinhala" **then** only tutors matching both filters are shown

- **Given** no tutors match my filters **when** I search  
  **then** I see a clear "no results, try broadening your filters" message instead of a blank screen

- **Given** I have applied filters **when** results load  
  **then** the first page returns within 800 ms at the 95th percentile

## INVEST self-check

- [x] **Independent** — Tutor searching can be implemented separately from booking and payment.
- [x] **Negotiable** — The search interface and filtering options can change based on user feedback.
- [x] **Valuable** — It helps students discover suitable tutors efficiently.
- [x] **Estimable** — Search requirements and performance expectations are defined.
- [x] **Small** — The story focuses only on finding tutors, not booking or payment.
- [x] **Testable** — Search results and response times can be measured.

# Story 3: Book a session with an available tutor

**As a** Student  
**I want** to book a 1-hour session with a tutor during one of their published slots  
**So that** I can get the help I need at a time that works for both of us  

## Acceptance Criteria

- **Given** a Tutor has an open slot **when** I select it and confirm  
  **then** the slot becomes "pending" and is no longer bookable by other students

- **Given** I have not completed payment **when** 10 minutes pass  
  **then** the slot is released back to the Tutor

- **Given** my booking is confirmed **when** I view "My Sessions"  
  **then** the session appears with date, time, and tutor name

## INVEST self-check

- [x] **Independent** — Booking has a clear purpose and can be developed as a separate workflow.
- [ ] **Negotiable** — The 10-minute payment hold rule is an assumption and needs founder confirmation.
- [x] **Valuable** — It allows students to secure tutoring sessions.
- [x] **Estimable** — Booking states, rules, and outcomes are defined.
- [x] **Small** — It only covers booking, not payment processing or session delivery.
- [x] **Testable** — Booking states and slot availability can be tested.

# Story 4: Pay for a session via card or eZ Cash

**As a** Student  
**I want** to pay for my booking using either a card or eZ Cash  
**So that** I can use whichever payment method is available to me  

## Acceptance Criteria

- **Given** I have selected a slot **when** I choose "Card"  
  **then** I am routed to the PCI-DSS-compliant gateway to complete payment

- **Given** I have selected a slot **when** I choose "eZ Cash"  
  **then** I receive a payment prompt on my registered mobile number

- **Given** payment fails for any reason **when** I return to the app  
  **then** I see the failure reason and can retry or choose a different method

## INVEST self-check

- [x] **Independent** — Payment processing can be separated from other system functions.
- [x] **Negotiable** — The exact payment provider implementation can change.
- [x] **Valuable** — It allows students to complete bookings successfully.
- [x] **Estimable** — Payment methods and expected behaviour are specified.
- [x] **Small** — It only focuses on payment completion.
- [x] **Testable** — Successful and failed payments can be tested.

# Story 5: Rate the other party after a session

**As a** Student (or Tutor)  
**I want** to rate the other party 1-5 stars with a one-line comment after a session  
**So that** future users can trust the marketplace based on real feedback  

## Acceptance Criteria

- **Given** a session has ended **when** I open the app within a reasonable window  
  **then** I am prompted to rate and comment

- **Given** I submit a rating **when** it is saved  
  **then** the other party's average rating is recalculated and shown on their profile

- **Given** I choose not to rate **when** the prompt window expires  
  **then** the session is simply left unrated with no negative consequence

## INVEST self-check

- [x] **Independent** — Rating functionality can exist separately from booking and payment.
- [x] **Negotiable** — Rating visibility and moderation rules can be adjusted.
- [x] **Valuable** — It improves trust between students and tutors.
- [x] **Estimable** — Rating requirements are clearly defined.
- [x] **Small** — The feature only handles reviews and ratings.
- [x] **Testable** — Rating submission and display can be verified.

# Story 6: Cancel a confirmed booking with notice

**As a** Tutor  
**I want** to cancel a confirmed booking as long as I give at least 12 hours' notice  
**So that** I have flexibility for genuine emergencies without unfairly stranding students  

## Acceptance Criteria

- **Given** a session is more than 12 hours away **when** I cancel it  
  **then** the booking is cancelled and the Student is notified immediately with a refund initiated

- **Given** a session is less than 12 hours away **when** I attempt to cancel  
  **then** the system blocks the cancellation and shows why

- **Given** I cancel within policy **when** the cancellation completes  
  **then** my public cancellation-rate stat is updated

## INVEST self-check

- [x] **Independent** — Cancellation has its own workflow and rules.
- [x] **Negotiable** — Cancellation rules can be changed after discussion.
- [x] **Valuable** — It protects students while allowing tutor flexibility.
- [x] **Estimable** — The cancellation process is clearly described.
- [x] **Small** — It focuses only on cancelling bookings.
- [ ] **Testable** — The cancellation-rate statistic is an assumption not defined in the original brief.

# Story 7: Receive weekly payout after commission

**As a** Tutor  
**I want** to be paid my earnings, minus the platform's 15% commission, via bank transfer every week  
**So that** I have predictable income I can rely on  

## Acceptance Criteria

- **Given** I completed sessions during the past week **when** the weekly payout run executes  
  **then** I receive 85% of total session value via bank transfer through Sampath Vishwa

- **Given** I want to check my earnings **when** I open my "Earnings" screen  
  **then** I see a breakdown of gross session value, commission deducted, and net payout per session

- **Given** a payout transfer fails **when** the failure is detected  
  **then** Ops Admin is alerted and I am notified of the delay

## INVEST self-check

- [x] **Independent** — Payout processing can be designed as a separate financial workflow.
- [x] **Negotiable** — Payment providers and payout schedules can be adjusted.
- [x] **Valuable** — Tutors need reliable access to their earnings.
- [x] **Estimable** — Commission and payout requirements are defined.
- [x] **Small** — It focuses only on tutor earnings and payouts.
- [x] **Testable** — Payout calculations and failure handling can be tested.
