# LearnLanka — Requirements Document

## 1. Problem Statement

Finding the right tutor for O/L and A/L studies can be challenging for Sri Lankan students due to differences in cost, language preference, availability, and tutor quality. Likewise, tutors often find it difficult to attract new students and manage bookings and payments efficiently. LearnLanka is an online tutoring marketplace designed to bridge this gap by connecting students with verified tutors through powerful search and filtering features. The platform also enables tutors to publish their availability, manage bookings, and receive secure payments, while LearnLanka handles scheduling, payment collection, and payouts to ensure a reliable and convenient experience for all users.

## 2. Personas

### Persona 1: The Student — "Javan, 17, doing A/L Combined Maths"
- **Goals:** Find a tutor who teaches in Sinhala at a price he can afford, book a session
  around his school and tuition-class schedule, and be confident the tutor is actually qualified.
- **Frustrations:** Existing options are word to word only; no way to compare tutors by price
  or rating before committing; unclear what happens if she needs to pay by eZ Cash instead of a
  card, since not everyone in her household has a bank card.

### Persona 2: The Tutor — "Mr. Jagath, part-time A/L Physics tutor"
- **Goals:** Fill unused evening hours with paid sessions, avoid no-shows and last-minute
  cancellations eating into his time, and get paid predictably every week without chasing
  parents for cash.
- **Frustrations:** No easy way to broadcast availability to many students at once; risk of
  being undercut or double booked; uncertainty about when commission is deducted and how much
  he actually receives.

### Persona 3: The Operations Admin — "Punchimanike, LearnLanka platform operations"
- **Goals:** Keep the platform trustworthy (verified tutors, fair disputes), make sure
  commission and payouts reconcile correctly every week, and respond quickly when something goes
  wrong (a payment fails, a session doesn't start, a rating dispute is raised).
- **Frustrations:** No visibility brief mentions into how disputes, refunds, or tutor
  verification are meant to work she has no defined tools or workflow for handling the
  exceptions that will inevitably occur.

## 3. Functional Requirements

### Student (Javan)

1. **FR-S1:** A Student can search for tutors filtered by subject, grade, language (Sinhala, Tamil, English), and price band.
2. **FR-S2:** A Student can view a tutor's profile, including rating and hourly rate, before booking.
3. **FR-S3:** A Student can book a 1-hour session with an available tutor.
4. **FR-S4:** A Student can pay for a booked session using either a card or eZ Cash.
5. **FR-S5:** A Student can rate a tutor from 1–5 stars and leave a one-line comment after a completed session.

### Tutor (Jagath)

1. **FR-T1:** A Tutor can publish one or more availability slots for students to book.
2. **FR-T2:** A Tutor can accept or decline an incoming booking request.
3. **FR-T3:** A Tutor can cancel a confirmed booking, provided at least 12 hours' notice is given before the session start time.
4. **FR-T4:** A Tutor can rate a Student from 1–5 stars and leave a one-line comment after a completed session.
5. **FR-T5:** A Tutor can view their weekly payout amount after the platform's 15% commission has been deducted.

### Platform / Operations (Punchimanike)

1. **FR-P1:** The platform automatically charges a 15% commission on every completed session.
2. **FR-P2:** The platform pays tutors their net earnings via bank transfer on a weekly cycle.
3. **FR-P3:** The platform captures and stores user consent for personal data processing at account creation, in line with data-protection requirements.
4. **FR-P4:** A user can submit a data-deletion request, and the platform must action it.

## 5. Assumptions

The project brief did not specify the following details. To enable the system design and requirements definition, the following assumptions have been made:

1. A tutoring session is considered **completed** (and therefore eligible for commission) once both the Student and Tutor have joined the video session and remained connected for at least **15 minutes**.

2. If a Tutor cancels a confirmed booking within the required 12-hour notice period or fails to attend the session, the Student is assumed to receive an **automatic full refund**.

3. The tutor verification process is not defined in the brief. It is assumed that an **Operations Administrator manually verifies** tutor identity and qualifications before the tutor is permitted to publish availability.

4. The process for resolving disputes (e.g., ratings, payments, or completed sessions) is not specified. It is assumed that the **Operations Administrator handles disputes manually**, and an automated dispute management system is outside the scope of Version 1.

5. There is no restriction stated on the number of bookings a Student may have. It is therefore assumed that a Student **may book multiple sessions with different tutors**, provided there are no scheduling conflicts.

6. If a Student's payment fails during the booking process, the booking is assumed to remain **pending for up to 10 minutes**. If payment is not completed within this period, the booking is cancelled and the tutor's availability is restored.

7. Tutor pricing is not defined in the brief. It is assumed that **each Tutor sets their own hourly rate**, allowing Students to filter tutors by price band.

8. While Tutors are required to provide at least **12 hours' notice** when cancelling a confirmed booking, the brief does not define an equivalent cancellation policy for Students. This is identified as an **outstanding business rule** that should be clarified in a future iteration.

## 6. Out of Scope (for this version)

The following features are explicitly excluded from Version 1 of the LearnLanka platform. While they may be considered for future releases, they are not part of the initial system implementation.

1. **Group Tutoring Sessions** – The platform will support only **one-to-one tutoring sessions** between a single Student and a single Tutor.

2. **In-App Messaging** – Students and Tutors will not have access to an integrated chat or messaging system before or after bookings.

3. **Native iOS Application** – Version 1 will focus on a **mobile-first web application** designed primarily for Android users. A native iOS application is outside the scope of this release.

4. **Automated Tutor Ranking Algorithm** – The platform will display tutor ratings based on the average of student reviews but will not implement an advanced ranking or recommendation algorithm.

5. **Session Packages and Subscription Plans** – Students will book and pay for individual tutoring sessions only. Discounted session bundles and subscription-based pricing are not included.
